# Report content, by chapter

One folder per Part/Chapter of the **agreed structure**, in the baseline document's own order.
The folder names mirror the agreed hierarchy exactly, so a section can always be located from its
number in the report.

**These folders are deliberately empty.** Section drafting has not started: the brief is to
complete the assessment first, then work Part by Part. Each folder is a placeholder for the work
in `../10-assessment/completion_matrix.csv`.

```
00-front-matter/            Foreword, FY2025/26 in Numbers, Executive Summary
01-about-rura/              Overview, mandate
02-strategic-plan/          Year-4 progress, scorecards, Action Plan, prior commitments
03-sectors/
  3.1-cross-sector/         Cross-sector overview
  3.2-ict/                  ICT, Broadcasting and Postal Services
  3.3-energy/               Energy
  3.4-water-sanitation/     Water and Sanitation
  3.5-transport/            Transport
  3.6-nuclear/              Nuclear Safety and Radiation Protection
04-regulatory-interventions/  Instruments, licensing performance, tariffs, compliance, UAF, case studies
05-consumer-protection/     Complaints and redress, education and awareness
06-governance-finance/      Board, corporate performance, stakeholders, financial performance
07-challenges-risks/        Risk register, sector challenges, institutional risks
08-outlook/                 FY2026/27 priorities, sector priorities, regulatory agenda
09-annexes/                 ICT licensing, statistical tables, financials, KPI table, instruments, definitions
```

## Rules for working in here

1. **The agreed structure is authoritative.** Do not add, merge, rename or reorder chapters or
   sections. Structural problems get flagged in
   `../10-assessment/05_structural_flags_and_length_control.md`, not fixed silently.
2. **Each sector keeps its five template headings** in this order: Sector Profile · Legal and
   Regulatory Framework · Licensing · Market Performance · Compliance Monitoring and Enforcement.
3. **Carry the status markers forward.** `MISSING`, `PARTIAL`, `CHECK`, `[DATA REQUIRED]` and
   `SOURCE TO CONFIRM` stay in the text until resolved — and resolution means answered, not deleted.
4. **Keep the dagger convention** for any calculated value, and record the formula in
   `../30-data/indicators/indicator_dictionary.csv`.
5. **Never invent a figure.** If the data has not arrived, the marker stays.
