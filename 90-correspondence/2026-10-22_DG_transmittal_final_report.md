## Note to the sender — not part of the email

The email above states that the document is final and confirmed. That is a fair
statement about **content**: the departments own their figures and have signed
them off.

It is worth being deliberate about **presentation**, because the review of 22
October found items that are visible on the page and that a reader will notice
before anything else:

- the right margin is 0.47 cm for almost the whole report
- four figure numbers are used twice and three are never used
- two annex tables appear in the List of Tables with no caption in the body
- four figure titles read "Electric Trend of Authorized Electric …"
- four placeholder captions still carry rows of dots, two of them printing the
  same title twice

If the DG forwards the attachment for printing, those go to print. Three options:

1. **Apply the fixes first,** then send the email with the bracketed sentence
   deleted. Roughly an hour's work on the `.docx`, and the cleanest outcome.
2. **Send now with the bracketed sentence kept.** The DG is told a production
   pass is still to come, and nothing in the email is overstated.
3. **Send now without it** — only if the document will not be printed or
   circulated further before the formatting pass.

Option 1 is the recommendation. Option 2 is sound if the approval is needed
today.
