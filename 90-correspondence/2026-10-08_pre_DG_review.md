## A · Blockers — visible in the first thirty seconds

**1. The cover still reads "WORKING DRAFT · DESIGN VERSION · based on the draft of 22 September 2026".**
Page 1, under the reporting period. If this goes to the DG described as the agreed document, the cover contradicts the covering message.

**2. The Table of Contents has not been generated.**
It currently prints: *"Right-click and choose Update Field to build the table of contents."* The same for the **List of Figures** and the **List of Tables** — three pages of instructions to the editor, immediately after the cover.
*Fix: Ctrl+A then F9 in Word — but only after all heading changes below, or it will need rebuilding.*

**3. Six empty headings will appear as blank lines in the contents.**
Lines with a heading style but no text, at ICT (2), Energy (2), Water (1), Transport (1).

**4. Six nuclear KPI items are formatted as Heading 1 — the same level as chapters.**
*"1. Protecting Patients, Workers, and Communities"*, *"2. Better Health Care Through Safe Licensing (23% increment)"* … through *"6. Investing in Rwandan Expertise (40%)"*. They will appear in the Table of Contents as if they were chapters of the report, between "Nuclear" and "Consumer Protection".

**5. The Consumer Protection chapter is an empty shell.**
It carries a title, an "IN THIS CHAPTER" list, and the sentence *"This chapter shows, for every sector, how many consumers complained, about what, and how quickly and fairly their complaints were resolved"* — then goes straight into the next chapter. No complaints content follows. The consumer material now sits inside the ICT and Water sections instead.
*Either move that content here, or remove the chapter and its promise.*

**6. Financial performance is promised but absent.**
The chapter is titled *"Corporate Governance, Stakeholder Engagement and Financial Performance"* and its contents list includes *"Financial performance"* — but no such section exists. No revenue, no budget execution, no audit opinion.
*Either the section arrives, or the chapter title and contents list should stop promising it.*

## B · Content

**7. Three chapters from the agreed structure are no longer in the document.**
Progress on the Strategic Plan 2022–2027 · Regulatory Interventions, Compliance and Enforcement · Challenges, Risks and Lessons Learned.
Losing the Strategic Plan chapter was already a known decision. Losing the other two is more consequential: *Regulatory Interventions* was where the year's licensing performance, tariff decisions and cross-sector compliance came together, and *Challenges and Risks* was the only place the report acknowledged what went wrong. Without them the report describes the sectors but says less about the Authority's own regulatory performance.
*Worth a conscious decision rather than an outcome — and worth telling the DG either way.*

## D · Design

**19. The six nuclear KPI items read as a different document.**
*"Better Health Care Through Safe Licensing (23% increment)"*, *"Ready Respond in an Emergency (80%)"* — campaign-style headings with percentages in brackets, in a report that is otherwise neutral and institutional. They are also the only place using this voice. Consider presenting them as the KPI cards used elsewhere, which would make them consistent and fix item 4 at the same time.

**20. Photograph rights are still unconfirmed.** Sector banner images appear to come from media sources with no stated owner or licence. For a national regulator this is a legal and reputational exposure, and it is the one item on this list that cannot be fixed after publication.

**21. The Standard's own pre-publication gate has not been run.** §17 requires the annotation count to reach zero. Seven remain (1 MISSING, 4 CHECK, 2 SOURCE TO CONFIRM) — close, and worth finishing rather than leaving visible.

**22. The editor's note and annotation legend should come out** with the annotations, once resolved.
