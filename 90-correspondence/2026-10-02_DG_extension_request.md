Dear [DG name],

Please find attached the draft FY2025/26 Annual Report, submitted as promised by the Data Analytics Team. The draft is complete in structure and covers all five regulated sectors and RURA's corporate performance. I am also writing to request **one additional week, to Friday 9 October**, to finalise it: a small number of figures are still being confirmed with the departments that own them, and we would rather validate them than publish them unverified.

**What the team has done.** The report has been rebuilt rather than updated. All five sectors now follow one structure, so they can be read and compared on the same terms. The reporting has moved from counting activity to showing outcome — what the issue was, what RURA did, and what changed as a result. Every headline indicator now carries its prior-year value, the change, and whether that change is an improvement, including where performance moved the wrong way. And every figure has been checked against its source rather than carried forward, which is what surfaced the discrepancies now being resolved.

The team has also produced a Visual Design Standard, so that charts, tables and indicators follow one system across this report and in future years.

**What the additional week is for.** The work remaining is validation, not drafting. Each item below has an owner and is already in progress:

- Financial performance — revenue, budget execution and the external audit opinion (Finance)
- The Foreword, with portrait and signature (Board Secretariat)
- Four figures awaiting departmental confirmation, in ICT, Transport, Water and Nuclear
- The register of regulatory instruments issued during the year (Legal)

**One matter for your guidance.** The chapter reporting progress against the Strategic Plan 2022–2027 is not in this draft, because the Year-4 targets and results were not available in time. As FY2025/26 is Year 4 of the Plan, we would prefer not to omit it. We recommend releasing the M&E Framework next week so Year-4 progress can be reported in full; the alternative is to state in the report that this assessment is deferred to FY2026/27.

On Friday 9 October you will receive the finalised draft, together with a short note on anything that could not be confirmed in time and why.
