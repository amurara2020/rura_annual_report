## Tier B — one house-style decision each, then mechanical

### B1. Fiscal year is written twenty different ways
`FY2024/25` (30), `FY 2025-2026` (26), `FY 2025/26` (18), `FY2025/26` (11),
`FY 2025/2026` (3) … plus fifteen more variants.

**Recommendation:** `FY 2025/26` throughout — space after FY, slash, two-digit
second year. Decide once and the whole document can be normalised in one pass.

### B2. Currency is written three ways
`Frw` (15), `FRW` (10), `RWF` (3), plus one `Rwandan Francs`.

**Recommendation:** `FRW` (the ISO code) throughout, and add it to the acronyms
list, where it is currently absent.

### B3. Licence vs license
`License/Licenses` (45 occurrences, American) against `Licence/Licences`
(16, British), plus lower-case variants — 108 instances in total, mixed.

Two defensible answers: standardise on **licence** (noun) / **license** (verb),
which is British English and the usual Rwandan government register; or
standardise on **license** everywhere, which is the current majority and the
smaller edit. This one is genuinely your call.

### B4. Other British/American splits
`authorisation` 1 vs `authorization` 15 · `programme` 6 vs `program` 10 ·
`fibre` 1 vs `fiber` 3 · `centre` 4 vs `center` 1.
One pass once B3 settles the register.

### B5. "Tariff" vs "Tariffs"
§2.2.2.2 is `Tariff`; the equivalent sections elsewhere are `Tariffs`
(2.1.2.3, 2.3.3, 2.4.1.3).
