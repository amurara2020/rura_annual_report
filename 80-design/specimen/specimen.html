Reading the tiles

Colour carries one meaning only: green — improved, red — worsened, amber — watch. The arrow gives the direction and the word repeats it, so the tile still reads correctly in greyscale, in print, and for a colour-blind reader. A rise is not automatically good: grid losses and outages rose, and both are marked as deteriorations.

Note: Changes are computed from figures published in this report.

Penetration passed 100

The dashed reference line marks 100 subscriptions per 100 inhabitants — the threshold the headline reports. Penetration counts active SIM subscriptions, so multi-SIM ownership can take it above 100; it is not a measure of how many people have a phone.

The bars keep the standard year pair — pale for the prior year, blue for the current year — on every two-year chart in the report. Deterioration is carried by the red change values and stated here, not by repainting the bars. Direction is in the geometry

Declines run left of the zero line and growth runs right, so the split is visible before any label is read. In the current edition every bar runs rightwards and direction is carried only by the colour and the minus sign — a −33.3% bar and a +58.3% bar point the same way.
