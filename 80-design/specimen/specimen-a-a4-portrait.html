3.3.4
Market performance Electricity reliability and the growth of demand

Reliability deteriorated on all three measured indicators in FY2025/26, while the customer base passed two million.

Installed generation capacity rose from 466.402 MW to 472.951 MW over the year, an increase of 1.4% †. Peak demand rose over the same period from 262.42 MW to 296.85 MW, an increase of 13.1%. Demand therefore grew at roughly nine times the rate of capacity.

Recorded outages rose from 1,675 to 1,917, and total outage duration from 1,342.97 hours to 1,838.3 hours — an increase of 36.9% †. Grid losses rose from 16.31% to 16.77%. The draft does not yet explain the causes of the additional outages.
